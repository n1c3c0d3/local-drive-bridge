Privacy Policy

Local Drive Bridge is a personal Google Drive integration used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public as a product or service.

Google data accessed

After the owner grants permission through Google OAuth, the local applications may access the owner’s Google Drive files and basic Google account identity information. Drive access is used only for operations initiated by the owner.

How data is used

Google Drive data is used to browse, mount, download, upload, organize, and manage the owner’s files through locally installed rclone and KDE software.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials, downloaded files, and temporary file caches are stored locally on the owner’s devices by the configured applications. This website does not operate an application server or database and does not receive Google user data.

Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties by the application owner. The local applications communicate with Google services as required to perform the owner’s requested Drive operations.

Removing access and data

The owner can remove access by deleting the configured Google account from KDE, deleting the rclone configuration, or revoking the app from the Google Account security settings. Locally downloaded or cached files can be deleted directly from the owner’s devices.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Website privacy

This informational website has no login, forms, analytics, cookies, or application backend.
