L Local Drive Bridge Privacy

Personal-use integration

Local access to one owner’s Google Drive

Local Drive Bridge is a private integration used by its owner to access Google Drive from locally installed Linux applications, including rclone and KDE KIO.

Purpose

The integration lets its owner browse, mount, download, upload, and manage their own Drive files from their own computer.

Availability

This is not a public service. It does not offer user accounts, file sharing, hosted storage, or remote access to visitors.

Data path

The local applications communicate directly with Google’s APIs after the owner grants permission through Google OAuth.

Privacy by design

No website tracking or data collection

This informational site has no login, forms, analytics, cookies, or application backend. Read the full privacy policy.
